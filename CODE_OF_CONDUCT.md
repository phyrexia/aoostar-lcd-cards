# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or sexualised content, in any channel of
  the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages).
- Do not publish other people's private information, and remove your own (host names,
  addresses, credentials) from logs before posting them.
- Help newcomers; a question asked twice is a sign the documentation can improve.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block people who
keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the
contact listed on the repository's profile page; reports are handled confidentially.
